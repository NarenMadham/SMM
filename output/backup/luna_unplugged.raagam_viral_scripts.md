# Script Pack Summary

## Recording Priority Order

### 1. Script 1 — “Love Songs or Breakup Songs?”

Record first because it has the simplest production setup, a clear debate mechanic, and direct alignment with the existing Love V/S BreakUp event identity.

### 2. Script 9 — “Old Songs or New Songs?”

Record second because it uses familiar Telugu music, creates strong identity-based debate, and can be filmed quickly with the two hosts.

### 3. Script 10 — “Send This to Your Singing Partner”

Record third because it is designed specifically for shares and tags, which are essential for reaching people outside the current follower base.

## Fastest Win

**Script 4 — “Guess the Song from the Humming.”**

It requires only one host, a recognisable melody, and simple editing. The delayed answer encourages rewatching, while the Telugu music reference makes the subject instantly clear.

## Longest Play

**Script 6 — “A Telugu Song Memory in One Question.”**

This can become a recurring community series featuring audience responses, hosts, performers, and attendees. Over time, it builds emotional association and positions @unplugged.raagam as a home for Telugu music memories, not only event announcements.

## Highest Risk, Highest Reward

**Script 7 — “Three Types of People at Unplugged Raagam.”**

It has strong tagging potential but depends heavily on natural acting, pacing, and host chemistry. If executed casually and humorously, it could attract non-followers through shares; if overacted, it may feel like generic promotional content.

## What Not to Do

1. **Do not begin with a poster, logo animation, or generic “August 8th event” announcement.** Start with the question, joke, contradiction, or song challenge.
2. **Do not invent venue, time, ticket price, setlist, crowd numbers, or attendee stories.** Confirm those details before adding them.
3. **Do not promise that the winning song will be performed unless the event team has approved that promise.**
4. **Do not use vague phrases such as “endless vibes” without showing specific proof: singing, audience response, requests, or interaction.**
5. **Do not use the same CTA in every Reel.** Rotate between voting, tagging, sharing, commenting, following, saving, and DMing for registration.
