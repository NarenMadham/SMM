## Format Mix Check

- **Reels:** Scripts 1–10 = exactly **10 Reels**
- **Carousels/comparison carousels:** Scripts 11–20 = exactly **10 Carousels**

## What Not to Do

1. Do not open discovery Reels with a logo, poster, venue exterior, or registration details.
2. Do not use unconfirmed song titles, venue information, prices, setlists, or timings.
3. Do not publish “Part 1” or “Part 2” without a separate, specific promise.
4. Do not make every post a generic “join us” announcement.
5. Do not use unrelated memes or celebrity bait that attract followers unlikely to care about Telugu sing-along events.
